# COMPLIANCE: Credit Portfolio Early Warning (Doc7)

Maps every catalog principle (P-01 to P-13) and every platform dependency rule (R1 to R8) to a
control in THIS repo, with the file that is the evidence. Aligned to MAS TRM, APRA CPS 234 and
CPS 230, HKMA and PDPA-class regimes; the regulatory crosswalk itself is adopter-owned (see the
last section).

## What this vertical is, in obligation terms

Post-origination credit monitoring produces a PROPOSAL about a borrower's classification. That
puts it in front of several regimes at once, and the shipped defaults are deliberately the
regulatory presumptions themselves rather than numbers somebody invented:

| Obligation | What this repo does about it |
|---|---|
| **IFRS 9 5.5.11** (the thirty-day rebuttable SICR presumption) and **B5.5.37** (the ninety-day default presumption) | The two shipped past-due clocks ARE those day counts. The engine raises `staging_backstop` and `presumption_rebuttable` as PRESUMPTIONS and routes to a human; only a human rebuts one. It proposes NO impairment stage and books no allowance, because the standard's primary test is a relative increase in lifetime probability of default that needs a PD model this repo does not have. |
| **CRR Article 178** and the **EBA definition-of-default guidelines**, with the absolute-plus-relative materiality shape of **Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2018/171** | The arrears materiality gate requires BOTH legs and runs before every past-due rule and every past-due floor. Both limits are configuration. `tests/unit/test_early_warning.py` pins the boundaries at 29/30/31, 89/90/91 and 179/180/181 days. |
| **EBA/GL/2020/06** (loan origination and monitoring): ongoing credit-risk monitoring, early-warning indicators, watchlist escalation | The whole vertical. Signals in four families, a watchlist proposal per obligor, and an escalation that is ROUTED rather than flagged. |
| **EBA/GL/2018/06** and the **ECB NPL guidance**: watchlist and unlikely-to-pay | `unlikely_to_pay` is set by the default presumption; the watchlist grade is proposed, never applied; exit from the watchlist exists (an upgrade) and is deliberately harder to earn than entry. |
| **MAS Notice 612** (this deployment's jurisdiction) | The classification vocabulary: pass, special mention, substandard, doubtful, loss. No internal watch rung is invented. |
| **MAS Notice 637** and **CRR Article 173**: at-least-annual grade review | `rating_review_max_age_days`, with an ABSENT review treated as a worse finding than a late one. |
| **BCBS 239**: risk-data aggregation and lineage | Every fired rule carries both the policy row it derives from and the source locator of the figure it fired on; the WORM record names the grade of record, the proposed grade, the composite and the applied floor rules, and exports with its hashes. |
| **SR 11-7** and **PRA SS1/23**: model risk | Stated plainly: a deterministic stdlib engine is STILL A MODEL. It needs a documented conceptual basis, ongoing outcome monitoring and independent validation. See the gap row below, and `model-risk-validation` for the fleet's validation service. |
| **EU AI Act Annex III** (creditworthiness assessment is high risk), **Article 14** (human oversight), **Article 12** (automatic event logging), and **GDPR Article 22** (no solely automated decision of this kind) | Nothing auto-executes: the grade registry has no write method in any profile, every consequential proposal routes to a named human with a stated approval count, and the hash-chained anchored trail is the automatic event log. |

**The strongest criticism a second-line reviewer will make, stated here rather than waited for:**
the weights, caps and band edges are ILLUSTRATIVE defaults, not a calibrated scorecard. A real
early-warning model is fitted on an institution's own default history and back-tested. This repo
ships no model card and no backtest of whether these signals predict deterioration.
`composite_score` ranks attention; it is not a probability of default, and a reader who takes it
for one has been misled.

## Adopter-owned crosswalk

The mapping above is to the catalog's own principles and rules. The mapping from those to a
specific regulation, and the decision that a control is SUFFICIENT for that regulation, is
adopter-owned: it depends on the institution's risk appetite, its regulator, its licence
conditions and its existing control library. This repo does not make that claim on an adopter's
behalf, and no row above should be quoted as regulatory assurance.

What an adopter is expected to add, in their own control library rather than in this file:

- the crosswalk from each row above to their MAS TRM, CPS 234, CPS 230, HKMA or PDPA control ids;
- the risk acceptance for every row still marked Partial or TODO at go-live;
- the second-line review of the deterministic policy in `domain/`, which is bank-owned logic and
  not a vendor default to be inherited unexamined;
- the retention schedule and the legal basis for the audit trail this service writes.
